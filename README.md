HTML5 and CSS3 virtual pet adoption website featuring multiple pages, pet information, care guides, an adoption form, and responsive web styling.


# Virtual Pet Adoption Website

A multi-page virtual pet adoption website developed as part of a Web Development Fundamentals project.

## Project Overview

The website is designed around a virtual pet adoption service and provides visitors with information about available pets, pet care, adoption, and the website itself.

The project includes:

- Homepage
- Pet information and adoption page
- Pet adoption form
- Form results/contact page
- About Us page
- Pet care information
- Embedded YouTube video
- Images of available pets
- Navigation between pages
- External CSS styling

## Features

### Homepage

The homepage introduces visitors to the virtual pet adoption service and provides information about pet adoption. It also includes an embedded YouTube video related to pet adoption. 

### Pet Information & Care

The pet information page presents four types of virtual pets:

- Dogs
- Cats
- Parrots
- Rabbits

Each pet is presented with its animal type, description, characteristics and image. The page also contains information about caring for each type of pet, including food, exercise, grooming, housing, health and general welfare. 

### Adoption Form

A dedicated form allows visitors to provide their details and select a pet they are interested in adopting.

The form includes:

- Name
- Email
- Age
- Birthdate
- Previous adoption experience
- Pet selection
- Written response about providing a good home
- Online community/challenge interests
- Story or image upload
- Submit and reset buttons

The pet selection includes dogs, cats, parrots and rabbits. 

### About Us & Contact

The project also contains an About Us page describing the purpose of the virtual pet adoption service and providing frequently asked questions. 

A contact/results page provides contact information and navigation back to the form and About Us page.
