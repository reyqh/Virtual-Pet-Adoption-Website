Information about the website

In our website we try to bring attencion and teach people abou the benefits of adopting a Pet Animals should never be viewed as property to be bought or sold. One of our main goals is to raise awareness and increase legislation surrounding the sale of animals for profit, and to promote animal adoption over purchase. So many helpless animals are available for adoption throughout the world, and every time an animal is purchased from a breeder, another loving animal sitting in a shelter cage remains without a loving home. With only a minor fee payable to the adoption agency, you could be the difference in giving that animal the opportunity they are longing for. Animals cant advocate for themselves, so we strive to be the voice they desperately need in cases of abuse or unethical practices.
